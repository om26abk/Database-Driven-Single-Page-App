# Persona

## Who is the user?
james, 19, is a first-year University of Hertfordshire student living in Hatfield. He has a tight budget, no car and a busy timetable. He used to play football at school but has done very little exercise since starting university.

## What does he need?
- Free or cheap ways to get active near campus
- Beginner-friendly options, because he feels nervous about gyms
- Information he can scan in a couple of minutes between lectures
- Student discounts and pay-as-you-go options
- Some support for his wellbeing and stress, not just fitness

## What frustrates him?
- Gym websites that hide prices or push long memberships
- Long articles when he only wants a name, a place and a cost
- Sites that are not usable on his phone
- Content aimed at families or experienced athletes, not students

## How does he use the web?
Mostly on his phone, in short visits of a few minutes, often while walking between buildings or waiting for a bus. He is comfortable with apps and social media but impatient with slow or cluttered sites.

## How will the site meet his needs?
- **Navigation:** filters for cost (free, pay as you go, student discount), activity type and location, so he can narrow down quickly.
- **Content shown:** posts about student discounts, free activities, local leisure centres, parks, beginner sport and wellbeing.
- **Content hidden:** WordPress boilerplate, legal pages, empty navigation pages and content aimed at families.
- **Visual style:** clean and calm, with large tap targets and short summaries, designed for phones first.
